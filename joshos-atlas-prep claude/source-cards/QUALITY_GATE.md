## Instructions

Before moving any card to approved-for-fable/:
1. Confirm the repo name, URL, and owner are correct
2. Confirm the sensitivity level is appropriate
3. Confirm there is no raw private content in the card
4. Confirm the evidence/source IDs exist in 04_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv
5. Mark low-confidence cards as excluded unless you can verify them

Low-confidence cards DO NOT go to Fable unless explicitly approved here.
